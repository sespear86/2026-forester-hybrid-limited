Standard on Limited Hybrid

Powertrain

  • 2.5L BOXER + electric motors · 194 hp
  • Lineartronic CVT, paddle shift, SI-DRIVE, EV mode
  • Symmetrical AWD · X-MODE Dual + Hill Descent
  • 8.7-inch clearance · 18-inch dark metallic machine-finish wheels
  • EPA 35/34/35 · up to 581-mile range

Safety

  • EyeSight + Automatic Emergency Steering
  • Adaptive cruise with lane centering
  • Blind-Spot Detection + Rear Cross-Traffic Alert
  • Reverse Automatic Braking
  • Rear camera · full airbag suite including knee bag

Comfort

  • StarTex, black or gray, blue stitching
  • 10-way power driver · 8-way power passenger
  • Heated fronts, mirrors, wiper de-icer
  • Dual-zone climate · panoramic moonroof
  • Rain-sensing wipers · one-touch folding rear seats
  • Wireless charger + USB-A/C front and rear

Tech and exterior

  • 11.6-inch nav · wireless CarPlay / Android Auto
  • 12.3-inch digital cluster · Harman Kardon 11-speaker
  • Hands-free power liftgate with height memory
  • Keyless access + push-button start
  • LED steering-responsive headlights + fogs
  • LED tube turn signals · chrome/black badges
  • Raised ladder roof rails (700-lb static)
