Limited Hybrid standard equipment

Powertrain

  • 2.5-liter BOXER engine with electric motors, 194 system horsepower
  • Lineartronic CVT with paddle-shift hybrid manual mode and SI-DRIVE
  • Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive
  • X-MODE Dual Mode with Hill Descent Control
  • EV Drive Mode and regenerative braking
  • 8.7-inch ground clearance
  • 18-inch dark metallic machine-finish alloy wheels, 225/55R18
  • EPA 35 / 34 / 35 mpg · up to 581-mile range

Safety

  • EyeSight with Automatic Emergency Steering and Emergency Lane Keep Assist
  • Adaptive cruise control with lane centering
  • Blind-Spot Detection with Rear Cross-Traffic Alert
  • Reverse Automatic Braking
  • Rear vision camera with dynamic guidelines
  • Frontal, side, curtain, and driver knee airbags

Interior

  • StarTex upholstery, black or gray, blue stitching
  • 10-way power driver seat with lumbar
  • 8-way power front passenger seat
  • Heated front seats, heated mirrors, windshield wiper de-icer
  • Dual-zone automatic climate control
  • Panoramic power moonroof
  • Rain-sensing wipers
  • One-touch folding rear seatbacks
  • Wireless phone charger
  • USB-A and USB-C ports, front and rear

Technology and exterior

  • 11.6-inch multimedia navigation
  • Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto
  • 12.3-inch digital gauge cluster
  • Harman Kardon 11-speaker audio
  • Hands-free power rear gate with height memory
  • Keyless access and push-button start
  • LED steering-responsive headlights, High Beam Assist, LED fog lights
  • LED tube-type front turn signals
  • Heated power mirrors with integrated turn signals
  • Raised ladder roof rails, 700-lb static load
  • Chrome and black exterior badging
  • Stainless steel tailpipe tip

Warranty

  • 3 years / 36,000 miles basic
  • 5 years / 60,000 miles powertrain
  • 8 years / 100,000 miles hybrid system
  • 5 years / unlimited miles rust perforation
  • 3 years roadside assistance
